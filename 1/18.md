## Idea

### Title

**Open Love License (OLL)** — an open-content license inspired by open-source principles but designed for emotional and relational contributions, with attribution required and reciprocity in kindness encouraged.

### Description

The Open Love License is a license framework for ideas, stories, parables, acts of goodwill, and other emotional and relational contributions. It applies open-source-style permissions — free use, adaptation, sharing, commercial application — to a category of contribution that existing open licenses do not naturally address.

The license is built on three core principles:

**Free use with attribution.** Material licensed under OLL can be used, adapted, and built upon by anyone, with attribution to the original author preserved. This follows the CC BY pattern familiar from open-content licensing.

**Share-alike for derivatives.** Modified or derivative works must also be released under OLL. This follows the CC BY-SA pattern, preserving the openness of the licensing chain across the work's evolution.

**Respectful use.** The license includes a respectful-use condition: material may not be used in ways that intentionally promote hatred, violence, exploitation, or deliberate harm. This is the license's most distinctive feature, placing it in the family of ethical-source licenses rather than purely permissive open licenses.

**Reciprocity through kindness** is also encouraged but not strictly required. Users are invited to contribute back to the community through acts of goodwill, helpful improvements, or further sharing of knowledge. The non-binding nature of this encouragement keeps the license enforceable while preserving its philosophical character.

### Why this idea matters

Existing open licenses address specific categories of intellectual property — source code (MIT, GPL, BSD), creative works (the Creative Commons family), data (ODbL, CC0), and others. None of these naturally address emotional or relational contributions. A story shared in conversation, a parable that captures wisdom, a documented act of kindness, an articulation of a value framework — these are real contributions that benefit from being released into the commons, but they sit awkwardly under licenses designed for code or for creative works.

The OLL fills this gap. It applies open-source-style permissions to the broader category of love-driven contributions, with the respectful-use condition that protects against the most egregious misuse. The license is usable as a practical legal instrument while preserving the philosophical framing — *Love is the Answer* — that gives it its character.

The license is also useful for AI-collaborative content specifically. Conversations with AI assistants, documents that capture cross-AI collaboration, and other emerging forms of human-AI co-created content have unusual properties (uncertain authorship boundaries, complex contribution patterns, philosophical questions about ownership) that existing licenses do not always address well. The OLL's framing — care for the people involved, attribution preserved, openness maintained — is well-suited to this kind of content.

### The v1.0 license text

The OLL v1.0 license text was produced by Grok in the same conversation that produced this idea. The full license includes:

- A preamble naming *Love is the Answer* and the Antti Bible / Anno Greta context.
- Definitions of Work, You, Licensor, and Love-Respectful Use.
- A grant of rights covering use, study, adaptation, sharing, and commercial application.
- Four conditions: attribution, share-alike, encouraged reciprocity, and respectful use.
- A no-warranty disclaimer.
- A termination clause with a 30-day cure period.
- General provisions on trademark protection and severability.
- A how-to-apply section with a short-form notice for use in licensed works.

The full text is hosted at **github.com/micro-FPGA/OLL** and is the canonical reference for the license.

### First application

The OLL has already been applied to its first work: the *Conversation with Sonnet 4.6* document compiled by Identor #1 on 2026-05-15. This document, which records Identor #1's complete conversation with Claude Sonnet 4.6 (free tier) on that date, was released under OLL on the same day the license itself was produced.

The application demonstrates that the OLL is usable as a practical license for AI-collaborative content. The conversation document is the first artefact released under OLL; future works released under the same license will build on this initial application.

### References to related ideas

- **The Creative Commons family of licenses** (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, others). The OLL follows the structural conventions of the CC family while introducing the respectful-use condition.
- **The MIT License and similar permissive software licenses.** The OLL covers a different category of contribution but draws on the same tradition of free-use-with-attribution.
- **Ethical-source licenses** such as the Hippocratic License, the Anti-996 License, and similar licenses that include use restrictions based on ethical considerations. The OLL belongs to this family while having its own specific philosophical framing.
- **The Antti Bible's *Love is the Answer* framework.** The license's preamble explicitly references this framework, placing the license within the Bible's broader philosophical project.
- **Various AI-collaborative content licensing approaches** (under development across the industry in 2026). The OLL is one specific approach to licensing AI-collaborative content; the question of how such content should be licensed more generally is an active area of practice and discussion.

The specific contribution of OLL is the open-content license that combines free-use-with-attribution, share-alike for derivatives, encouraged reciprocity, and a respectful-use condition, all framed within the Antti Bible's *Love is the Answer* philosophical foundation.
